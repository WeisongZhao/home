Principal Investigator

Weisong Zhao (赵唯淞), Ph.D.

Weisong Zhao is a tenured Professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Instrumentation Science and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT). He received his B.S. degree in Physics in 2017 and Ph.D. degree in Instrumentation Science and Technology in 2022, both from HIT. His research focuses on advanced microscopy for biomedical applications, with particular interests in super-resolution imaging and intelligent image reconstruction and analysis across imaging modalities. All related to biomedical imaging. He has published first- and corresponding-author papers in Nature, Nature Biotechnology, Nature Photonics, and Nature Methods, etc. He also serves as an Associate Editor of npj Imaging | Nature. He has authorized/applied more than 20 Chinese invention patents and PCT international patents.

Find more details in his curriculum vitae -> CV

Associate Investigator

Liying Qu (曲丽颖), Ph.D.

Liying Qu is an Associate Professor at the HIT under the Chunyan Talent Program. She earned her Ph.D. in Instrument Science and Technology from the HIT in 2025. Her research lies at the intersection of machine learning and super-resolution fluorescence microscopy, with a strong focus on their biological applications. Her work has been published in journals, including first-author publications in Nature Methods and Clinical and Translational Medicine. She is a recipient of Qingjin Award from the Chinese Laser Press (2024).
